Privacy

Your diary is private by default. Entries and media are visible only to the signed-in owner. AI features send only the entry or audio you explicitly choose to Google’s Gemini service when you press an AI button. The tagline appears here as a reminder:

“You are nothing but a number of days; whenever a day passes, a part of you is gone.”— Imam Hasan al-Basri

Your data

Export downloads a JSON file of your entries. “Delete all” removes entries only; delete photos and voice memories from the Media Vault.

About MyDimension

A private, single-owner AI diary & memory vault. Frontend: HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (PWA). Backend: Python FastAPI. Storage: PostgreSQL + the owner’s private Google Drive.
